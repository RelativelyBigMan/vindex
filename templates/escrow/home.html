Abstract

Rental deposits are usually held by one side of the agreement. safeDeposit holds the tenant's deposit in escrow for the whole lease instead. When the lease ends, the tenant, the owner and an independent arbiter each vote, and any two matching votes decide where the deposit goes. No single party can release it alone.

The protocol

(Escrow)

The owner opens an escrow with the lease dates, the deposit amount and the wallets of all three parties.

(Deposit)

The tenant pays the deposit in {{ escrow_config.currency.code|default:"stablecoin" }} from their own wallet. It stays locked for the lease.

(Decision)

At the end of the lease each party votes d = tenant or d = owner.

The release rule

Let vp be the vote of party p, one of tenant, owner or arbiter. Count the votes for an outcome d:

votes(d) = |{ p : vp = d }|

(Two of three)

The deposit is released to d as soon as more than one party agrees:

votes(d) > 1 → release(d)

If tenant and owner agree, it's settled with no arbiter needed. If they disagree, the arbiter's vote decides. Every vote is signed in the voter's own wallet.†
