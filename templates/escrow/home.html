Abstract

Rental deposits are usually held by one side of the agreement. safeDeposit locks the tenant's deposit in an escrow on the XRP Ledger instead. If nothing goes wrong, the ledger itself hands the deposit back when the lease ends. If there is a dispute, the money moves to an account that only two of the tenant, the landlord and an independent arbiter can spend from together. The ledger returns your deposit, not us.

How it works

(Lock)

The tenant locks the deposit in a token escrow. Its destination is a fresh settlement account whose own key is switched off.

(Live)

During the lease nobody can spend the deposit: not the tenant, not the landlord, not safeDeposit. Anyone can check it on the ledger.

(Return)

After the lease end plus a grace period g, the escrow can only go back to the tenant. A bot submits the return; anyone else could too.

The settlement rule

Let S be the set of parties who signed a proposed split p, each one of tenant, landlord or arbiter. The settlement account pays out only when

|S(p)| ≥ 2 → pay(p)

(Automatic return)

If the escrow is still locked when the ledger's time t passes the lease end e plus the grace period g, only one thing can happen to it:

t > e + g → return(tenant)

If tenant and landlord agree, it's settled with no arbiter needed. If they disagree, the arbiter's signature decides. The arbiter is verified by a credential on the ledger.†
