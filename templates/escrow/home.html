Rental deposits nobody can run off with

The tenant's deposit sits in escrow for the length of the stay. When the lease ends, the tenant, the owner and an independent arbiter vote, and any two of them decide where the money goes.

How it works

1. Set up

The owner creates the escrow with the lease dates, the deposit amount and the wallet addresses of all three parties.

2. Deposit

The tenant pays the deposit in {{ escrow_config.currency.code|default:"stablecoin" }} into escrow. Nobody can move it alone.

3. Decide

When the lease ends, each party votes to return the deposit to the tenant or release it to the owner. Two matching votes settle it.
